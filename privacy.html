Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Nivaz (“we”, “us”) is an app for finding, booking and living in hostels, PGs, co-living spaces and 1 RK homes in Hyderabad. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. If you have any question, write to us at SUPPORT_EMAIL_TO_BE_CONFIRMED.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number — to log you in with a one-time SMS code. We don't use passwords.
  • Your name, and whether you are a working professional, student or family — shown to owners when you book or ask to visit.
  • An emergency contact number — only if you choose to add one.
  • Your bookings, visits and stays — which property, room and bed, move-in and move-out dates.
  • Rent bills and payments — amounts, dates, and the UPI reference numbers you type in. You pay owners directly from your own UPI app; Nivaz does not receive or hold your money and never sees your bank or card details.
  • Things you send in the app — complaints (and any photo you attach), meal skips and food ratings, requests (late entry, night out, guest visit, notice to vacate).
  • For owners and their staff — property details, floor plans, the UPI ID residents pay to, menus, notices, expenses and bill photos.

We do not use advertising or tracking tools, and we don't collect your location, contacts or other phone data.

Why we use it

Only to run Nivaz: logging you in, showing available beds, handling bookings, visits and stays, rent bills and receipts, complaints, food and requests, and helping owners manage their properties. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The owner (and their staff, such as the warden) of a property you ask to visit, book or live in can see your name and what you've sent them. Your phone number is shared with the owner only after they confirm your visit or booking, and the owner's contact is shared with you at the same time.
  • Other residents cannot see your information.
  • Service providers we use to run the app: Supabase (our database and file storage, kept in Mumbai, India), and Twilio (to send the SMS login code to your number). The app also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google can see your device's internet address.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid request from the police or a court.

How we keep it safe

All connections are encrypted. Access rules in our database make sure each person only sees what they are allowed to: residents see their own records, owners see their own properties, and photos are private and opened only through short-lived links.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: tap your name at the top → Delete my account.
  • On the web: Delete your Nivaz account (log in with your mobile number).

Deleting removes your login for good and erases your name, phone number, emergency contact and complaint photos. Owners keep the records they need for their accounts and the law (past stays, rent bills and payments), shown as “Deleted user”. While you are still living at a property, have a booking in progress, owe rent or are owed a refund, the app will ask you to finish that first.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. Records of stays, rent and payments are kept for as long as owners need them for their accounts and as required by law, without your name or phone number after you delete your account.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal information, and raise a complaint. You can correct your name and emergency contact yourself in the app (tap your name at the top). For anything else, write to SUPPORT_EMAIL_TO_BE_CONFIRMED and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Nivaz is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with your parent or guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
