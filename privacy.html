Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Nivaz (“we”, “us”) makes two apps: Nivaz, for finding, booking and living in hostels, PGs, co-living spaces and 1 RK homes in Hyderabad, and Nivaz Partner, for the owners and staff who run them. This policy covers both. It explains, in plain words, what information the apps collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. If you have any question, write to us at nivaz.support@gmail.com, or call or WhatsApp us on +91 63014 26330.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number — to log you in with a one-time SMS code. We don't use passwords.
  • Your name, and whether you are a working professional, student or family — shown to owners when you book or ask to visit.
  • An emergency contact number — only if you choose to add one.
  • Your bookings, visits and stays — which property, room and bed, move-in and move-out dates.
  • Rent bills and payments — amounts, dates, and the UPI reference numbers you type in. You pay owners directly from your own UPI app; Nivaz does not receive or hold your money and never sees your bank or card details.
  • Your location, only if you allow it — the Nivaz app asks after you log in. (Nivaz Partner doesn't ask.) It is used on your phone to show how far each stay is from you and to sort stays by distance. It stays on your phone: it isn't sent to us, and owners never see it. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Your ID for the owner, only if you live at a PG on Nivaz and choose to add it — a picture of your masked Aadhaar (only the last 4 digits show), a passport-size photo and your permanent address. Hyderabad police ask PGs and hostels to register their residents; your owner uses these for that police verification. We never ask for your full Aadhaar number.
  • Things you send in the app — complaints (and any photo you attach), meal skips and food ratings, requests (late entry, night out, guest visit, notice to vacate).
  • Stays you save — the stays you tap the heart on, so you can find them again.
  • Reviews you write — star ratings (overall, food, cleanliness, Wi-Fi) and a comment about a place you have lived in.
  • Cashback and referrals — your referral code, whose code you entered when you signed up, the cashback you have earned (from meal skips, friends who booked, and owners you brought to Nivaz), and the UPI ID you give us for receiving cashback. When we pay you, we keep the amount, the date and the UPI reference number.
  • For owners and their staff (in Nivaz Partner) — property details, photos of the property, floor plans, the property's map location (saved to about 100 metres, so customers can see how far it is; an owner can fill it in from their phone's location while at the property), the UPI ID residents pay to, menus, notices, replies to reviews, expenses and bill photos. Owners can add residents who already live at their property, with the resident's name, mobile number, bed, rent and move-in date, so the stay is ready in the resident's app when they log in with that number. Owners also tell us when they pay Nivaz's monthly fee (the amount, the month and the UPI reference number).
  • The team that runs a property (in Nivaz Partner) — the owner, and managers the owner adds, can add the people who help run the property: managers, and staff such as the warden, cook, housekeeping, security or accountant. For each person we keep their name, mobile number, role and job, and who added them, so that their Nivaz Partner app opens that property when they log in with that number.
  • Your language in Nivaz Partner — the language you pick (English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada or Marathi) is saved on your phone only, not sent to us.

We do not use advertising or tracking tools. We don't collect your contacts or other phone data. Your location never leaves your phone, except when an owner chooses to save it as their property's map location.

Maps. The maps in the apps show OpenStreetMap data, loaded from OpenFreeMap's map servers (openfreemap.org). To draw the part of the map you are looking at, your phone asks those servers for that area, as any web map does; this shows them your internet address but not your account, and your location isn't sent. “Directions” opens Google Maps, where Google's privacy policy applies.

Why we use it

Only to run Nivaz: logging you in, showing available beds, handling bookings, visits and stays, rent bills and receipts, complaints, food and requests, reviews, paying cashback, and helping owners manage their properties. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The owner of a property you ask to visit, book or live in, and the managers the owner has added there, can see your name and what you've sent them. Your phone number is shared with them only after they confirm your visit or booking, and the property's contact is shared with you at the same time. The property's staff (such as the warden or cook) see what they need for their work: complaints, requests, meal counts and notices, with residents' names and rooms. They don't see rent, payments or your ID.
  • Your ID (masked Aadhaar picture, photo and permanent address) can be seen only by you and the owner of the property where you live or have a confirmed booking (and the managers the owner has added there). Not other residents, not the property's staff, and not the Nivaz team. The owner can still see it for 90 days after you move out, in case the police ask; after that it is deleted.
  • A property's team: the owner and its managers see the names and mobile numbers of the people on its team. Staff don't see the team list.
  • Everyone using Nivaz can see the reviews you write, with your first name only and how long you stayed. The owner can reply to a review, and the reply is shown under it. Property photos are public too, as part of the listing.
  • Other residents cannot see your other information. Your saved stays are seen only by you.
  • A friend whose referral code you enter doesn't see your name or number; they see only how many friends joined and booked. If someone enters your code, they see your first name. If an owner joins Nivaz Partner with your code, you see their first name when your ₹1000 cashback is added.
  • The Nivaz team (the Nivaz admin, and Nivaz Support staff who help owners and check payments) sees your cashback, your payout UPI ID and the friends who joined with your code, so we can check and pay cashback. It also sees owners' monthly Nivaz fee payments (the amount, the month and the UPI reference number) to confirm them. Nivaz Support can't change fees or anyone's role.
  • Service providers we use to run the app: Supabase (our database and file storage, kept in Mumbai, India), and Twilio (to send the SMS login code to your number).
  • Other apps you choose to open: if you tap Call, WhatsApp, Share or a map link, that app opens and its own privacy policy applies.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid request from the police or a court.

How we keep it safe

All connections are encrypted. Access rules in our database make sure each person only sees what they are allowed to: residents see their own records, owners and managers see their own properties, and staff see only their property's day-to-day work. Complaint and bill photos and ID pictures are private and opened only through short-lived links. Floor plans (which beds are free, in which room) are shown only to people logged in with their mobile number.

Surveys

When you take part in our research survey, we store your answers without your name, ID or Aadhaar number. The survey doesn't ask for a UPI ID or mobile number: any thank-you payment is made to you directly, outside the survey. If an owner leaves a phone number or Gmail (to test the app, for a follow-up call or the owner pilot), we keep it apart from the answers, use it only for that, and delete it when the research ends. Only the Nivaz admin can see survey answers. The survey is for people aged 18 and above.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete my account.
  • On the web: Delete your Nivaz account (log in with your mobile number).

Deleting removes your login for good and erases your name, phone number, emergency contact, ID pictures and permanent address, complaint photos, saved stays and payout UPI ID. Cashback not yet paid is cancelled. Owners keep the records they need for their accounts and the law (past stays, rent bills and payments), shown as “Deleted user”; reviews you wrote stay on the listing as “Former resident”. While you are still living at a property, have a booking in progress, owe rent or are owed a refund, the app will ask you to finish that first.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. Your ID pictures and permanent address are deleted 90 days after you move out (or sooner if you delete them or your account). Records of stays, rent and payments are kept for as long as owners need them for their accounts and as required by law, without your name or phone number after you delete your account.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct or erase your personal information, and raise a complaint. You can correct your name and emergency contact yourself in the app (Profile → Personal info), change or replace your ID in Profile → Your ID for the owner, and change your payout UPI ID in Profile → Rewards. For anything else, write to nivaz.support@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Nivaz is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with your parent or guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
